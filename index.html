How To Play

          
    
            
  1. Go to Home and choose an AI game or a player-made game.
    2. 
            
  2. Click Play on any game card.
    3. 
            
  3. On desktop: use W/A/S/D to move, mouse to look, and Space to jump.
    4. 
            
  4. Use Left Click to remove blocks and Right Click to place blocks.
    5. 
            
  5. Press 1 / 2 / 3 to switch block type.
    6. 
            
  6. On mobile: use the on-screen movement + action buttons in Play mode.
    7. 
            
  7. Progress is saved automatically between sessions.
